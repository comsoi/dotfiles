Ctrl+Shift+W\x1b[87;6u大写 W=87
Ctrl+Tab\x1b[9;5uTab=9，5 = 1 + Ctrl
Ctrl+Shift+Tab\x1b[9;6u6 = 1 + Shift + Ctrl
